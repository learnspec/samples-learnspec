## Q4 · What time signature is typically associated with a waltz?

- [ ] 4/4
- [x] 3/4
- [ ] 6/8
- [ ] 2/4

> Waltzes are in 3/4 time — three quarter-note beats per measure, giving the characteristic "ONE-two-three" feel.

## Q6 · Which of the following are correct about the C major scale?

```quiz
type: multi
points: 2
difficulty: medium
```

- [x] It uses only natural notes (no sharps or flats)
- [x] It starts and ends on C
- [ ] It contains an F#
- [x] The half steps are between E–F and B–C
- [ ] It has a melancholic sound

> [!correct] C major uses all white keys on a piano, with half steps at E–F and B–C.
> [!incorrect] C major has no sharps or flats, and major scales have a bright (not melancholic) sound.
