## Q5 · Fill in the blank: Earth's axial tilt of approximately ___ degrees creates the seasons.

```quiz
difficulty: easy
```

**Answer:** 23.5

> Earth's axial tilt of about 23.5° causes different parts of the planet to receive varying amounts of sunlight throughout the year, producing seasons.

## Q6 · True or false: The Sun is the largest star in our galaxy.

- [ ] True
- [x] False

> The Sun is a medium-sized G2V star. Many stars in the Milky Way are far larger — for example, UY Scuti has a radius about 1,700 times that of the Sun.
